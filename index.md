I am a **Postdoctoral Research Associate** at the Homer L. Dodge Department of Physics & Astronomy, **The University of Oklahoma**. I work on the **kinematics and dynamics of galaxies**, combining physical models, statistics and scientific software to measure circular and non-circular motions in gaseous disks with integral-field and interferometric 3D spectroscopy.

I was born and raised in Ticul, Yucatán, Mexico, and obtained my MSc and PhD in astrophysics at the Instituto de Astronomía of the Universidad Nacional Autónoma de México (IA-UNAM), working on galactic winds and jets with Dr. Sebastián F. Sánchez. In 2021 I joined the Academia Sinica Institute of Astronomy and Astrophysics (ASIAA) in Taiwan as a Distinguished Postdoctoral Fellow, and in 2025 I moved to the University of Oklahoma.

I develop and maintain open-source software for kinematic modelling, most notably [XookSuut and XookSuut3D]({{ '/code/' | relative_url }}), which are used across several surveys and research projects. I also enjoy teaching and mentoring students on galaxy dynamics and star formation.
